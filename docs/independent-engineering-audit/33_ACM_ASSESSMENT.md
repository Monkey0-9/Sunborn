## 💻 Computer Science Foundations & Algorithmic Rigor

1. **Algorithms & Data Structures**:
   - SIMD-accelerated 64-bit Roaring Bitmaps (`CompressedLineageGraph`): demonstrates state-of-the-art spatial complexity ($\approx 1.8 \text{ bytes/edge}$) and microsecond transitive closure evaluation over power-law knowledge graphs.
   - Merkle Trees (RFC 6962): provably collision-resistant inclusion proofs for agent tool graphs.
   - Radix Trees: prefix cache routing for LLM KV cache affinity.
2. **Distributed Systems Theory**:
   - Monotonic Epoch Lease Fencing: strictly prevents stale writeback anomalies under concurrent mutations.
   - Vector Clocks: causal ordering across distributed pods under asynchronous network delays.
   - Non-Accelerator Authority: establishes formal separation between authoritative state (WAL) and ephemeral accelerators.
3. **AI Safety & Statistical Computing**:
   - Non-Exchangeable Conformal Prediction: rigorous mathematical bounds on false-hit risk ($\alpha \le 0.001$).
   - Wilson Score Intervals: statistically robust coverage estimation under small sample regimes.
